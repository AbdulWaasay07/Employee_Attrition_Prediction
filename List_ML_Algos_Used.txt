1. MACHINE LEARNING ALGORITHMS (CORE PREDICTIVE LAYER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1. XGBoost Classifier (xgboost.XGBClassifier)
* Type: Supervised Machine Learning (Gradient Boosted Decision Trees / Ensemble Learning).
* Objective / Task: Binary Classification & Continuous Probability Scoring (0.0 to 1.0 / 0% to 100%).
* Business Purpose: Predicts the precise probability that an employee will leave the organization within the next 6 to 12 months.
* Hyperparameters & Configuration:
  - n_estimators = 100 (Builds 100 sequential boosting trees)
  - max_depth = 3 (Controls tree depth to prevent overfitting on tabular HR data)
  - learning_rate = 0.1 (Shrinkage factor for robust convergence)
  - eval_metric = 'logloss' (Binary logistic loss function)
  - random_state = 42 (Ensures deterministic, reproducible training runs)
* Input Features (21 Dimensions):
  tenure_years, years_since_last_promotion, promotion_velocity, manager_tenure_years, 
  salary_growth_rate, total_compensation, avg_weekly_hours, avg_overtime_hours, 
  workload_stress_index, sick_leave_ratio, remote_work_ratio, total_hr_complaints, 
  high_severity_tickets, days_since_last_complaint, avg_hr_satisfaction, 
  latest_performance_rating, avg_performance_rating, rating_trend, 
  trainings_completed, training_score_avg, employee_satisfaction_score.
* Output: Continuous probability scores stored in employee_features.attrition_risk_score.

1.2. K-Means Clustering (sklearn.cluster.KMeans)
* Type: Unsupervised Machine Learning (Centroid-Based Distance Partitioning).
* Objective / Task: Workforce Persona Segmentation (k = 3 cohorts).
* Business Purpose: Automatically groups employees into intuitive behavioral personas for HR managers.
* Hyperparameters & Configuration:
  - n_clusters = 3 (3 distinct workforce risk personas)
  - n_init = 10 (10 centroid initialization runs selecting the best inertia)
  - random_state = 42 (Reproducible clustering)
* Input Dimensions (4 Core Signals):
  tenure_years, avg_overtime_hours, salary_growth_rate, latest_performance_rating.
* Centroid Profiling & Persona Mapping:
  - "High Performers (Low Risk)": Cluster centroid with highest performance rating and normal working hours.
  - "Burnout / Flight Risk": Cluster centroid with highest overtime hours and elevated workload stress.
  - "Underutilized / Stagnant": Cluster centroid with lowest salary growth rate and stagnant tenure without advancement.
* Output: Categorical persona stored in employee_features.employee_segment.

2. FEATURE ENGINEERING & MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS (THE 24 FEATURES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1. Z-Score Feature Standardization (sklearn.preprocessing.StandardScaler)
* Formula: z = (x - mu) / sigma
* Purpose: Standardizes multi-dimensional metrics to zero mean (mu = 0) and unit variance (sigma = 1) before K-Means distance calculations.

2.2. Workload Stress Index
* Formula: Workload Stress Index = avg_weekly_hours + (avg_overtime_hours * 1.5)
* Purpose: Amplifies overtime hours to capture non-linear burnout risks.

2.3. Salary Growth Rate (Compensation Velocity)
* Formula: Salary Growth Rate = (salary_latest - salary_first) / salary_first
* Purpose: Measures career compensation progression against tenure.

2.4. Promotion Velocity
* Formula: Promotion Velocity = Total Promotions / tenure_years
* Purpose: Quantifies advancement speed per year of service.

2.5. Rating Trend (Performance Trajectory)
* Formula: Rating Trend = latest_performance_rating - avg_performance_rating
* Purpose: Detects sudden performance declines or upward career momentum.

2.6. Derived Employee Satisfaction Score (0 to 100 Scale)
* Formula: 
  Satisfaction = clip(50 + (10 * Rating) + (20 * TrainingRatio) - (8 * Complaints) - (15 * HighSevTickets) - (0.5 * OvertimeHours), 0, 100)
* Purpose: Synthesizes multi-domain sentiment into an aggregate workforce morale indicator.

2.7. Estimated Replacement Cost
* Formula: Replacement Cost = Annual Base Salary * 0.50
* Purpose: Industry benchmark quantifying the financial loss of employee turnover.

2.8. Complete 24-Feature Vector Roster:
  1. tenure_years: (Current Date - hire_date) / 365.25
  2. years_since_last_promotion: Years elapsed since latest promotion
  3. salary_growth_rate: Compound wage growth percentage
  4. total_compensation: salary + bonus + stock_options
  5. avg_weekly_hours: Mean weekly hours worked
  6. avg_overtime_hours: Mean weekly overtime hours logged
  7. workload_stress_index: Combined workload strain metric
  8. sick_leave_ratio: Total sick leaves / tenure months
  9. remote_work_ratio: Remote working days / total working days
  10. total_hr_complaints: Total HR grievances submitted
  11. high_severity_tickets: Count of high-severity friction tickets
  12. days_since_last_complaint: Recency of workplace friction
  13. avg_hr_satisfaction: Average CSAT rating on resolved tickets
  14. latest_performance_rating: Most recent review rating (1 to 5)
  15. avg_performance_rating: Historic mean appraisal rating
  16. rating_trend: Trajectory delta between latest and historic rating
  17. trainings_completed: Count of completed upskilling programs
  18. training_score_avg: Mean assessment score across courses
  19. manager_tenure_years: Duration under current reporting manager
  20. promotion_velocity: Promotions per year of tenure
  21. employee_satisfaction_score: Composite sentiment index (0-100)
  22. attrition_risk_score: Continuous ML flight risk probability (0.0-1.0)
  23. estimated_replacement_cost: Financial turnover replacement impact ($)
  24. employee_segment: Qualitative persona cohort string

3. STATISTICAL & DATA CLEANING TECHNIQUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1. Statistical Winsorization (99th Percentile Quantile Capping)
* Technique: pandas.Series.quantile(0.99) combined with .clip(upper=limit)
* Purpose: Caps extreme compensation and workload outliers ($999,999 typos) to protect statistical integrity without deleting rows.

3.2. Central Tendency Median Imputation
* Technique: .fillna(df[col].median())
* Purpose: Imputes missing numerical cells using median values to resist outlier skew.

3.3. Categorical Imputation & String Standardization
* Technique: Whitespace stripping, title-casing (.str.title()), and missing value substitution with "Unknown".

3.4. Regular Expression Currency Parsing
* Technique: Regex pattern r'[^\d\.-]' stripping '$', commas, and text from financial inputs.

3.5. Fuzzy Boolean Coercion
* Technique: Parses mixed truth representations ('true', '1', 'yes', 'completed') into strict boolean primitives (True / False).

3.6. Safe Datetime Parsing & Conversion
* Technique: pd.to_datetime(errors='coerce') and conversion to native Python datetime objects for database compatibility.

3.7. Cryptographic UUID Primary Key Generation
* Technique: uuid.uuid4() generation for missing primary identifiers.

3.8. Synthetic Boundary Sampling
* Technique: Injects synthetic positive/negative boundary samples during edge-case training to guarantee stable binary target calibration.

4. DATA ENGINEERING & RELATIONAL DATABASE TECHNIQUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1. Chunked Stream Ingestion (chunksize = 10,000)
* Purpose: Streams large CSV files in 10,000-row chunks to ensure constant low memory (RAM) usage during file processing.

4.2. Smart Upserting & Primary Key Deduplication
* Technique: Bulk pre-check and delete on incoming primary keys combined with row-by-row session.merge() fallback.
* Purpose: Enables safe re-uploading of datasets without MySQL 1062 duplicate key crashes.

4.3. Relational Foreign Key Integrity Enforcement
* Architecture: Central primary hub (employees) linked to 5 transactional satellite tables (compensation, performance_reviews, workload_attendance, hr_tickets, training_engagement).

4.4. Binary Model Serialization (Joblib)
* Artifacts: Serializes trained weights to .joblib files (attrition_model.joblib, segmentation_kmeans.joblib, segmentation_scaler.joblib, attrition_features.joblib) for sub-millisecond API inference.
